# Curriculum: Arithmetic (age 6)

## Season

1. Numbers to 100: count, compare, make ten.
2. Addition and subtraction within 20.
3. Shapes and measurement.

## Current topic

Make ten: the pairs that add up to 10.

## Steps

1. Ten-frame: ten cells, filled left to right.
2. "How many more make ten?" for 9, 8, 7, 6, 5.
3. Add within 10 using make-ten: 8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3.
4. Say the pairs from memory: 9+1, 8+2, 7+3, 6+4, 5+5.

## Next

Count by twos to 10.
