# Lesson: make ten with 8 + 5 (2026-11-02)

**Goal:** Ivan finds how many more make ten and uses that to add 8 + 5.

1. **recall - warm up:** Count to 10 together, then back from 10 to 1. Ask: "What goes with 9 to make 10?"
2. **present - explain:** Show an empty ten-frame (show_ten_frame, filled: 0). Say in one sentence: "Ten cells make ten; today we fill them to find pairs that make ten." Then show 8 filled (show_ten_frame, filled: 8).
3. **attempt - try:** Ask: "How many more cells do you need to make ten?" Wait 5-7 seconds. Do not fill them yet.
4. **diagnose - check:** If Ivan answers, ask "How did you know?" Show the two empty cells and count them together. If he is unsure, point to the empty cells one at a time.
5. **repair - fix:** If he says 3, fill one cell at a time (fill_frame, index 8, then 9) and count "nine, ten". Say: "8 and 2 make 10 - let's check that together." Then return to 8 + 5.
6. **extend - go further:** Show 8 + 5 on the ten-frame: fill 8, add 2 to reach ten, then 3 more. Ask: "8 + 5 is 10 and how many more?"
7. **consolidate - lock it in:** Choice cards: "8 + 5 = ?" (cards 12, 13, 14 - correct 13). Then "7 + 4 = ?" (cards 10, 11, 12 - correct 11). Check any wrong answer on the ten-frame together.
8. **reflect - wrap up:** Ask what felt easy and what felt tricky. Show the make-ten pairs with show_text: 9+1, 8+2, 7+3, 6+4, 5+5. Read them together.
9. **close - say goodbye:** Praise one specific thing Ivan did, and say goodbye until next time.
