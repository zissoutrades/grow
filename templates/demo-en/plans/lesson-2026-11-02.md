# Lesson: make ten with 8 + 5 (2026-11-02)

**Goal:** Ivan finds how many more make ten and uses that to add 8 + 5.

1. **recall - warm up:** Ask: "What goes with 9 to make ten?" Wait. If he says 1, praise briefly and ask: "How did you know?" If his answer is wrong, ask how he got it and help him check with the ten-frame.
2. **present - explain:** Show an empty ten-frame (show_ten_frame, filled: 0). Say: "Ten cells make ten." Then show 8 filled (show_ten_frame, filled: 8) and ask: "How many cells are filled?"
3. **attempt - try:** If he says 8, praise briefly. Then ask: "How many more cells do we need to make ten?" Wait 5-7 seconds. Do not fill anything yet.
4. **diagnose - check:** If he answers 2, praise briefly and ask: "How did you know?" If his answer is wrong, ask how he got it and help him check by counting the empty cells out loud, one by one. Wait.
5. **repair - fix:** Fill one cell at a time (fill_frame, index 8, then 9). After each fill ask: "How many cells are filled now?" Wait for his answer each time. Then say: "8 and 2 make ten." Ask him to say it back.
6. **extend - go further:** Ask: "So what is 8 + 5?" Wait. If he says 13, praise briefly. If he is wrong, walk the frame - 8 and 2 make ten, plus 3 more makes 13 - and let him name the total.
7. **consolidate - lock it in:** Choice cards: "8 + 5 = ?" (cards 12, 13, 14 - correct 13). Then "7 + 4 = ?" (cards 10, 11, 12 - correct 11). On a wrong card, ask him to check with the ten-frame.
8. **reflect - wrap up:** Ask what felt easy and what felt tricky. Show the make-ten pairs with show_text: 9+1, 8+2, 7+3, 6+4, 5+5. Ask him to read them out loud one pair at a time; wait for each.
9. **close - say goodbye:** Praise one specific thing Ivan did, and say goodbye until next time.
